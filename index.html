Kids learn about money by handling it. But cash pocket money disappears without a trace. Nobody remembers what was +$10 given, what was −$3 spent, or what was $10 saved.

So you become the family bank, and Pocket Cub keeps the record. Your child gets their own view of it on the same phone, so money becomes something they can see, count and make choices about.

Set up
Kid Mode

01

Set up your family

Sign in with your phone number or Google, name your family and pick a parent passcode.

Works with US (+1) and Indian (+91) numbers.

02

Add your child

Set a starting balance and an allowance, and choose whether their money earns interest (monthly, weekly or every few days) or stays as a simple balance.

Tap a colour on the phone. Each child gets their own.

03

Hand over the phone

Switch to Kid Mode. Your child sees their money, logs what they spend and ticks off their chores.

Kid Mode has its own soft mint look, so it’s always clear whose turn it is.

04

Every dollar, on the record

Set each child’s pocket money and starting balance. Every dollar in and out is recorded as an entry, so there’s always a clear history.

05

Chores that pay

Give each child chores with a reward: one-off, daily or weekly. When your child marks a chore done, the reward lands in their account straight away.

Tap a chore on the phone. Optional reminders nudge them at a time you choose.

06

Log a spend

When your child buys something, they log it themselves and see their balance before and after. Made a mistake? They can undo it right away.

Pick something and log it on the phone.

    Parents ask.
    Cub answers.

    Is real money moved through the app?

    No. Pocket Cub is a shared record between you and your child. You hand over cash or pay as you normally would, and the app keeps track.

    Does my child need their own phone?

    No. Kid Mode runs on your phone, and a passcode keeps Parent Mode locked.

    Who pays the interest?

    You do. You decide the rate and the lock period, a bit like being your child’s own bank.

    Can I have more than one child?

    Yes. Add as many as you like, each with their own account.

    What age is it for?

    Roughly 5 to 14: kids learning to save, spend and earn with real numbers, not lectures. It works in US dollars and other currencies, and you can sign in with a US (+1) or Indian (+91) number, or Google.

    Is it free?

    Yes. No card and no fees.

    Which phones does it work on?

    Android now, iPhone coming soon.
